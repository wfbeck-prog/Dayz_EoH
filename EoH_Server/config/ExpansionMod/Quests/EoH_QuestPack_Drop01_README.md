# EoH Quest Pack Drop 01

This is the first production quest drop for Echoes of Humanity.

## Scope

Drop 01 targets the first 40 quests in the full 200 quest rebuild.

The pack is organized around four arcs:

1. Signal and Ashes - early survivor/intel chain.
2. Supply Lines - logistics, recovery, and delivery chain.
3. Blood on the Roads - combat pressure and route security chain.
4. Black Market Rumors - illicit intel, drug economy, and dangerous contact chain.

## Design Rules

- EoH tone: grim, post-collapse, grounded survival.
- Rewards must be worth doing.
- No direct punch card rewards.
- Punch cards stay tied to Tier 4/CBD/bunker progression.
- Bunker-tier weapons should not be common quest rewards.
- Rewards may include money, ammo, meds, drug economy supplies, mid-tier weapons, and intel items.
- High-end quests can point players toward bunker/black market content without bypassing it.
